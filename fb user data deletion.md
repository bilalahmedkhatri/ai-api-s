# User Data Deletion Instructions

**Effective Date:** September 15, 2026

If you wish to stop using our application and want your data completely and permanently removed from our systems, please follow the instructions below. We respect your right to privacy and data deletion.

## Method 1: Remove App Integration via Facebook (Recommended)

When you remove our application from your Facebook Business Integrations, your Facebook Access Token is immediately invalidated, and we can no longer access your Page or schedule posts.

To do this, please follow these steps:
1. Log in to your Facebook account.
2. Go to your **Settings & Privacy** > **Settings**.
3. In the left-hand menu, scroll down and click on **Business Integrations** (or "Apps and Websites", depending on your Facebook layout).
4. Find our application in the list of active integrations.
5. Click **Remove** next to the application name.
6. A confirmation box will appear. You may optionally check the box to delete any posts the app may have published on your behalf, then click **Remove** again to confirm.

*Note: Once you complete this step, our system will automatically detect the invalidation of the token and queue your stored data (such as Page IDs and encrypted tokens) for permanent deletion within 30 days.*

## Method 2: Request Manual Data Deletion via Email

If you prefer to request manual deletion, or if you want to ensure your data is purged immediately without waiting for the 30-day automated cycle, you can submit a manual request.

1. Send an email to **bilalahmedkhatri@outlook.com**.
2. Please use the subject line: **"Data Deletion Request"**.
3. In the body of the email, include:
   * Your full name as it appears on Facebook.
   * The URL or name of the Facebook Page(s) you connected to our app.
   * A clear statement that you wish for all your associated data to be deleted.

We will process your request and securely delete all your access tokens, Page identifiers, and related scheduling data from our databases within **48 to 72 hours**. You will receive a confirmation email once the deletion is complete.

---

### What Data Gets Deleted?
When a deletion is processed, we permanently remove:
* Your encrypted Facebook Access Tokens.
* Your connected Facebook Page IDs and Names.
* Any scheduled posts that have not yet been published.

If you have any questions about this process, please contact us at bilalahmedkhatri@outlook.com.
