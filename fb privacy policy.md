# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** September 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to our application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application to manage and schedule automated posts on your Facebook Pages.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide our automated posting and scheduling services, our application collects and uses essential user data, which includes:
* **Public Profile Information:** Basic information from your Facebook profile required to authenticate your identity.
* **Page Identifiers:** The IDs and names of the Facebook Pages you connect to our application.
* **Access Tokens:** Secure tokens granted by Facebook that allow our application to act on your behalf to publish and schedule posts.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information **solely for the purpose of managing and scheduling automated posts on your behalf**. Specifically, your data is used to:
* Authenticate your account and verify your permissions for the connected Facebook Pages.
* securely communicate with the Facebook Graph API to publish content to your Pages at the scheduled times.
* Provide basic application functionality and customer support.

We do **not** use your data for advertising, and we will **never share, sell, or rent your information with third parties**.

## 4. Data Security
We are committed to protecting your user data using robust security measures. Your Facebook Access Tokens are encrypted using industry-standard symmetric encryption (Fernet) before being stored in our database. We employ secure communication protocols (HTTPS) to ensure your data remains safe from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
We retain your data only for as long as you actively use our application to schedule posts. 

**How to Request Data Deletion:**
If you wish to stop using our application and have your data completely removed from our systems, you can request data deletion at any time. 
* Please contact us at **bilalahmedkhatri@outlook.com** with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
* Alternatively, you can remove our application's access directly from your Facebook account settings (Settings > Security and Login > Business Integrations). When you remove the integration, we will delete your associated access tokens and Page identifiers from our database within 30 days.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
